## 3. MAKALE 1 — Mineral: TALK

### Takeaway
Talk, Mg3Si4O10(OH)2 formüllü, T-O-T tabakalı bir fillosilikattır; Mohs sertliği 1 olan en yumuşak mineraldir, doğal olarak hidrofobiktir ve kâğıt, boya, plastik, seramik, kozmetik gibi alanlarda dolgu/kaplama malzemesi olarak kullanılır.

### Cited Findings
- Kimyasal formül: Mg3Si4O10(OH)2; fillosilikat; tetrahedral silisyum ve oktahedral magnezyumdan oluşan T-O-T tabakası; tabakalar arası zayıf van der Waals bağları yumuşaklığın kaynağıdır — [Talk-PDF](https://mdpi-res.com/d_attachment/minerals/minerals-09-00668/article_deploy/minerals-09-00668-v2.pdf)
- Siloksan yüzeyi nedeniyle talk hidrofobiktir — [Talk-PDF](https://mdpi-res.com/d_attachment/minerals/minerals-09-00668/article_deploy/minerals-09-00668-v2.pdf)
- Özellikler (makalenin girişi): düşük sertlik, kimyasal kararlılık (asitle tepkimeye girmez), yüksek adsorpsiyon, düşük plastisite, düşük ısıl/elektriksel iletkenlik — [Talk-PDF](https://mdpi-res.com/d_attachment/minerals/minerals-09-00668/article_deploy/minerals-09-00668-v2.pdf)
- Kristal sistemi: monoklinik veya triklinik (uzay grubu C2/c veya C-1); grup: pirofillit-talk grubu — [Wikipedia: Talc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Talc)
- Mohs sertliği: 1 (ölçeğin tanımlayıcı minerali); özgül ağırlık 2,58–2,83; renk: açık-koyu yeşil, kahverengi, beyaz, gri, renksiz; parlaklık: mumsu/sedefsi; çizgi rengi beyaz; dilinim: {001} boyunca mükemmel (bazal); kesilebilir (sektil); yarı saydam; habit: yapraksı-lifsi kütleler — [Wikipedia: Talc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Talc)
- Makalede yoğunluk hesaplarında 2,8 g/cm3 alınmıştır — [Talk-PDF](https://mdpi-res.com/d_attachment/minerals/minerals-09-00668/article_deploy/minerals-09-00668-v2.pdf)
- Kullanım alanları: kâğıt, boya, kauçuk, seramik, refrakter, polimer sanayisinde kaplama/katkı/dolgu; kimya ve ilaç sanayisi yüksek saflıkta ve tekdüze tane boyutlu talk ister — [Talk-PDF](https://mdpi-res.com/d_attachment/minerals/minerals-09-00668/article_deploy/minerals-09-00668-v2.pdf)
- Tane boyutuna göre kullanım: 44 µm seramik ve boya; 8–12 µm kâğıt; 7 µm kozmetik; ≤2 µm kauçuk. Nano boyutlu talk: nanokompozitlerin ısı direncini artırma ve atık su filtre malzemeleri — [Talk-PDF](https://mdpi-res.com/d_attachment/minerals/minerals-09-00668/article_deploy/minerals-09-00668-v2.pdf)
- Talk kalitesi saflık, beyazlık ve tane boyutuna göre sınıflandırılır — [Talk-PDF](https://mdpi-res.com/d_attachment/minerals/minerals-09-00668/article_deploy/minerals-09-00668-v2.pdf)
- Talkça zengin kaba kayaç sabuntaşı (steatit) olarak adlandırılır; ısıya dayanıklılığı nedeniyle soba, lavabo, laboratuvar tezgâhı ve elektrik panolarında kullanılır — [Wikipedia: Talc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Talc)
- Dünya üretimi: Çin başlıca üreticidir (2016'da yaklaşık 2,2 milyon ton, dünya üretiminin %30'u); talk-karbonat ultramafikleri Türkiye'nin ofiyolit kuşaklarında da bilinir — [Wikipedia: Talc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Talc)
- Türkiye: MTA yayınlarında Sivas-Hafik Virancık köyü talk yatakları ve Balıkesir-İvrindi Gümeli talk yatağı (ayrıca Balıkesir İvrindi-Havran-Edremit talk prospeksiyonu) anılmaktadır (yalnızca arama sonucu özetinden; belgelerin tam metni açılamadı) — [MTA bülteni](https://bulten.mta.gov.tr/dosyalar/makaleler/655/tr_20241231101938_655_1_3f2ec9e5.pdf); [DergiPark](https://dergipark.org.tr/en/pub/ogummf/article/325728)

### Takeaway

### Cited Findings
- **Abstract (verbatim):** "The properties and preparation of talc have long been investigated due to its diverse industrial applications, which have expanded recently. However, its comminution behavior is not yet fully understood. Therefore, having better control of the particle size and properties of talc during manufacturing is required. In this study, we investigate the effect of the ball size in a high-energy ball mill on the comminution rate and particle size reduction. High-energy ball milling at 2000 rpm produces ultrafine talc particles with a surface area of 419.1 m2/g and an estimated spherical diameter of 5.1 nm. ## 6. MAKALE 2 — Mineral: BARİT

### Takeaway
Barit (BaSO4), ortorombik sistemde kristallenen, yüksek yoğunluklu (yaklaşık 4,5 g/cm3), kimyasal olarak inert bir sülfat mineralidir; dünya üretiminin büyük kısmı petrol-doğal gaz sondaj çamurlarında ağırlaştırıcı olarak kullanılır. ## 8. MAKALE 2 — Özet (aynen) ve önemli şekil/tablolar

### Takeaway

### Cited Findings
- **Abstract (verbatim):** "Gravity separation tailings generated during barite ore beneficiation represent a potentially valuable secondary source of barite, particularly when BaSO4 occurs in fine fractions poorly recovered by conventional gravity separation. This study investigated barite recovery from gravity separation tailings of monobarite ore from the Shiganak deposit and developed an integrated gravity-flotation flowsheet. Mineralogical, X-ray phase, gravimetric, and particle-size analyses showed that the tailings consisted predominantly of barite and quartz and contained approximately 42.5% BaSO4, with a substantial proportion concentrated in the finest fraction. Open-circuit flotation tests identified a grinding fineness of 95% passing 0.071 mm, together with 800 g/t Na2CO3, 200 g/t Na2SiO3, and 240 g/t oleic acid, as the best-performing conditions among those investigated. Under closed-circuit conditions, a barite concentrate containing 91.50% BaSO4 was obtained at 75.19% recovery and a 34.92% yield. The BaSO4 content exceeded the minimum value of 90% specified for KB-3 under GOST 4682–84. Qualitative foam tests indicated reduced apparent activity of residual flotation reagents after thermal treatment at 250 °C for 20 min and, separately, after treatment with 0.10% sodium tripolyphosphate. Combining the separately determined gravity and flotation balances resulted in a projected overall BaSO4 recovery of 90.17% for the proposed integrated process, compared with 60.37% for gravity separation alone. The proposed gravity–flotation approach enables additional barite recovery from current and accumulated tailings, reducing mineral losses and tailings storage requirements." — [Barit-PDF](https://mdpi-res.com/d_attachment/minerals/minerals-16-01070/article_deploy/minerals-16-01070.pdf)
